    Our school

    A happy placeto learn

    Veer New Look Central School, Halol opened in 2005. It is run by Manibhadra Veer Education Trust and is part of the New Look Group of Institutions.

    We aim for the all-round development of every child. Our youngest learn through play. Older students learn by doing: in the science and maths labs, in the library, and on educational tours.

    Questions parents ask

    Ask us anything

    Q.Which board is the school affiliated to?
    A.

    CBSE. Our affiliation letter and other CBSE documents are on the Mandatory Disclosure page.

    Q.Which classes do you have?
    A.

    Nursery to Class 12. Pre-primary has Nursery, LKG and UKG.

    Q.What is the medium of teaching?
    A.

    English. The school is co-educational.

    Q.What documents do we need for admission?
    A.

    For every class except LKG and UKG, your child needs the original Transfer Certificate from the last recognised school. [FULL DOCUMENT LIST TO CONFIRM]

    Q.How is Class 11 admission decided?
    A.

    On the Class 10 Board result and a short interview. We offer Science and Commerce. [PCM / PCB DETAILS TO CONFIRM]

    Q.Where can I see the fees?
    A.

    The fee structure is published with our CBSE Mandatory Disclosure. For anything else, call the office on 02676 225955.
